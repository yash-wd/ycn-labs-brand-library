# Placement, print and special situations — requires proof

Digital: maintain intrinsic ratio; use responsive `object-fit: contain` rather than cover; prefer source resolution adequate for CSS render width × display pixel ratio. No export can show more detail than its source pixels. For BRAND-EXPORT-05 opaque-first, the 6675 px RULING-01 master is the source of the full-logo exports and candidate crop proposals; approved transparent variants will use their own approved masters.

Business stationery: full-color imagery needs 300ppi at final size for high-quality raster printing. At 300ppi, the 6675px-wide master corresponds to about 22.25 inches/56.5cm width, and a 2048px export to about 6.83 inches/17.3cm, subject to printer/process. Large signage needs an approved high-scale reconstruction or responsible raster scaling with real proofs, not a claim of lossless infinite zoom.

Embroidery/laser engraving/embossing: glows and thin constellation details may be physically unmanufacturable. Ask for a separately approved one-color production/optical adaptation and proof on the target material. Do not silently simplify official source.

Favicons/app icons: 16–48px cannot represent this full wordmark and micro-line structure legibly. Produce a small-size mark only with explicit Owner authorization; do not claim it is a faithful full-logo export.

Motion: do not animate/redesign the base artwork without Owner authorization; if allowed, define animated logo asset separately from the static brand source.
